## Participant information

Dear Participant,

Thank you for taking the time to complete this questionnaire.

This survey aims to explore the relationship between students’ study habits (such as study hours, class attendance, revision practices, note-taking methods, and phone distractions) and their academic performance.

Your responses will help us better understand how different study behaviours may relate to academic outcomes.

Please note:

The survey is anonymous. No personal identifying information will be collected.

Your participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time.

There are no right or wrong answers — please respond honestly based on your own experience.

The questionnaire will take approximately 4–6 minutes to complete.

By continuing, you consent to participate in this study.

Thank you for your valuable contribution!
